NEVER STOP is a single-player, offline arcade driving game. There is no account, no network use, no in-app purchases and no user-generated content, so no login or demo account is needed.

HOW TO PLAY
After the loading screen the title screen shows three mode cards (Pursuit, Sky Arena, Rooftop Rumble); the selected one starts with PLAY.
- Pursuit: escape the police for as long as you can. You are only caught ("busted") when police cars box you in and you stop.
- Sky Arena / Rooftop Rumble: ten cars on a platform high above the city; push the others off, last car on it wins.
Touch controls (landscape): slide a thumb on the left half to steer. On the right half, press to accelerate, drag down to brake or reverse, drag up for nitro, drag sideways to drift. JUMP / DROP buttons appear when those Workshop parts are fitted. Pause is at the top left. A first-run coach teaches the controls, and How to play on the title screen explains the rules. Game controllers and keyboards also work.
Garage, Records and Settings are on the title screen. Cash is earned only by playing; there are no real-money purchases.

CONTENT
The city, cars, police, sound and music are original, made by Musa Studios (largely procedurally). Built with the Godot Engine and Jolt Physics (MIT licence); the Outfit typeface is under the SIL Open Font Licence. Credits are under Settings > Credits & licences. Pedestrians always step out of the way and cannot be hit; only vehicles crash.
