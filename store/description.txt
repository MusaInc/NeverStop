NEVER STOP — THE POLICE WILL CATCH YOU. MAKE THEM WORK FOR IT.

One car. One city. Every police unit in town on your tail.

You start with a single patrol car behind you. Every minute more join in: interceptors cutting you off from ahead, rammers lining up PIT manoeuvres, heavy 4x4s, roadblocks with spike strips and a helicopter overhead, until the whole city is after you. You're never caught because one car touched you. You're caught when they box you in. So keep moving.

DRIVE IT YOUR WAY
• Thread traffic, drift corners, take the jump ramps and cross the bay on the suspension bridge.
• Knock down street lights, bins, signs and trees. Ram police cars off the road for takedowns.
• Lose panels as you crash, then drive through a green repair beacon to fix it all.
• Chain near misses, drifts, air and takedowns for big scores.

TWO BATTLE MODES
• Sky Arena: ten cars on a floating platform high above the city. Last car on it wins.
• Rooftop Rumble: ten cars on steel decks across the tallest towers, joined by girder bridges.

BUILD YOUR GETAWAY CAR
• Ten cars, from a hot hatch to a seven-tonne truck, each with its own perk.
• Upgrade engine, grip, armour and nitro. Pick your paint and wheels.
• Workshop: ram bars, armour plating, spiked wheels, hydraulics, caltrops and mines.

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO CHASE
• Rolling contracts in every run, 22 challenges and 11 career ranks.
• A daily streak bonus, personal records and your recent runs.
• A full day–night cycle and changing weather.

PLAY IT ANYWHERE
• Made for touch, with a smart one-thumb stick or pedals, plus controller support.
• iPhone and iPad.
• Fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no in-app purchases.
