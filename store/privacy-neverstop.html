NEVER STOP privacy policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

NEVER STOP is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad made by Musa Studios.

What we collect: nothing. The game has no account or login, does not send anything over the internet, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Your game progress and settings (cash, cars, upgrades, records, challenges and preferences) are stored only on your device, by the game itself. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children: the game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes: if a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact: Musa Studios, support@musastudios.co.uk
